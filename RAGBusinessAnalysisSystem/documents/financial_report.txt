Company: Microsoft
Document Type: Financial Report Summary
Reporting Period: Fiscal Year 2024
Source: https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar24/index.html

Microsoft reported revenue of approximately $245.1 billion
for fiscal year 2024.

Microsoft operates across three main reporting segments:
Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud,
and More Personal Computing.

Microsoft's business includes Microsoft 365, Azure,
Windows, LinkedIn, Dynamics, gaming, and other software
and cloud services.

Azure and other cloud services are part of Microsoft's
Intelligent Cloud business segment.

Financial figures in this document refer to fiscal year 2024.
Do not interpret them as current-year financial results.
